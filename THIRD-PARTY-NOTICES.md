## LGPL replacement and corresponding source

JExcelApi, Logback, and Lanterna are unmodified and distributed as separate
JARs in `lib/`. The launcher uses `lib/*`; recipients may replace these JARs
with interface-compatible builds for relinking or debugging. No Maven shade or
assembly step merges them into `analyzer.jar`.

- JExcelApi source: https://sourceforge.net/projects/jexcelapi/files/jexcelapi/2.6.12/
- Logback Classic source: https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/ch/qos/logback/logback-classic/1.5.18/logback-classic-1.5.18-sources.jar
- Logback Core source: https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/ch/qos/logback/logback-core/1.5.18/logback-core-1.5.18-sources.jar
- Lanterna source: https://repo1.maven.org/maven2/com/googlecode/lanterna/lanterna/3.1.2/lanterna-3.1.2-sources.jar

The LGPL-2.1, LGPL-3.0, and GPL-3.0 texts are included under `licenses/`.
Nothing in this notice changes the terms of the applicable upstream license.

## Components not bundled

The Oracle JDBC driver and the CUBRID database engine shared library are not
included in the distribution. They must be installed or supplied separately
when those runtime modes are used.
